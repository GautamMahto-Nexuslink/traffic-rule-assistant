14.3 Green Transportation Initiatives 
●​ Public Transport Promotion: 
○​ Bus Rapid Transit: Dedicated bus corridors. 
○​ Metro Rail: Underground and elevated rail systems. 
○​ Integrated Transport: Seamless connectivity. 
○​ Last Mile Connectivity: Feeder services. 
●​ Non-Motorized Transport: 
○​ Cycling infrastructure and pedestrian facilities. 
○​ Integration with public transport. 
○​ Safety measures for vulnerable users.
