8.1.2 Vehicle Related Violations 
●​ Driving Unregistered Vehicle: ₹10,000-₹15,000; vehicle seizure; imprisonment up to 3 
months. 
●​ Driving Without Insurance: ₹2,000 (first offense), ₹4,000 (subsequent); imprisonment up to 
3 months. 
●​ Using Vehicle Without Permit: ₹10,000; permit cancellation. 
●​ Overloading: 
○​ Passenger vehicles: ₹20,000 per excess passenger. 
○​ Goods vehicles: ₹20,000 + ₹2,000 per ton excess weight. 

●​ Pollution Certificate Violation: ₹10,000; vehicle registration cancellation possible.
