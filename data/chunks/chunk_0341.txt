5.2.3 Informatory Signs (Rectangular) 
●​ Distance Markers: Information about destinations. 
●​ Service Signs: Fuel stations, hospitals, hotels. 

●​ Tourist Information: Heritage sites, attractions. 
●​ Emergency Services: Police stations, hospitals, first aid posts. 
●​ Parking, Public Telephone, Eating Place, Resting Place, Light Refreshment.
