4.3 Traffic Police Hand Signals

If the traffic is being controlled and directed by the traffic police
personnel, follow their hand signals even if they are different
from traffic lights or signals as there may be an emergency
situation on the road. Following pictures illustrate different hand
signals used by the traffic police as shown in the diagrams from
16to21.
