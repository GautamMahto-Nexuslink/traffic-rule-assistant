6.1.2 Commercial Licenses 
●​ Class LMV: Light Motor Vehicle (taxis, auto-rickshaws). 
●​ Class MGV: Medium Goods Vehicle. 
●​ Class HGV: Heavy Goods Vehicle. 
●​ Class PSV: Public Service Vehicle (buses). 
●​ Class TRANS: Transport Vehicle License. 
●​ Class HGMV: Heavy Goods and Medium Goods Vehicle. 
●​ Class HPMV: Heavy Passenger Motor Vehicle.
