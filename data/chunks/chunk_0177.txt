6.3.1 Renewal Process 
●​ Timeline: Can be renewed up to 1 year before expiry; grace period of 1 year after expiry with 
late fee. 
●​ Documents: Medical certificate for commercial licenses. 
●​ Fee Structure: Varies by license category (₹200 for non-transport, ₹1,600 for transport). 
●​ Note: After 1 year of expiry, a fresh application is required.
