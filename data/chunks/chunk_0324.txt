4.1.1 Right of Way Rules 
●​ General Rule: Traffic on the main road has right of way over traffic from side roads. 
●​ Roundabouts: Traffic already in the roundabout has right of way. 
●​ Pedestrian Crossings: Vehicles must yield to pedestrians at designated crossings. 
●​ Emergency Vehicles: All vehicles must give way to ambulances, fire engines, and police 
vehicles with sirens. 
●​ Uncontrolled Intersections: Vehicles from the right have priority.
