In case there is an indication of engine overheating, switch off
any unnecessary vehicle equipment like air conditioning. Pull
over to the sideof the road, stop the vehicle and turn the engine
off immediately. Raise hood if it is safe to do so to allow the engine
to cool down. Do not open the radiator cap. Driving the vehicle
with an overheated cooling system can damage the engine.
