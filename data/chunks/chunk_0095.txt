Section 112 of Motor Vehicles Act 1988 stipulates that 'no person
shall drive a motor vehicle or cause or allow a motor vehicle to be
driven in any public place at a speed exceeding the maximum
speed or below the minimum speed fixed for the vehicle under the
Act or by any other law for the time being in force.'

Your speed must not be more than 25 km/h, if you are passing a
procession, troops or police on the march or when passing
workmen engaged in road repair work.
