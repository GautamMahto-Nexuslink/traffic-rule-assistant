13.1.1 Immediate Actions 
●​ Stop Immediately: Do not leave accident scene. 
●​ Check for Injuries: Provide first aid if qualified; don’t move seriously injured persons. 
●​ Call Emergency Services: Police (100), ambulance (108), fire service (101), integrated 
emergency (112). 
●​ Secure the Scene: Prevent further accidents; place warning triangle 50 meters behind.
