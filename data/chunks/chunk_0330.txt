4.2.3 Helmet and Seatbelt Requirements 
●​ Motorcycle Helmets: Mandatory for rider and pillion passenger; must meet ISI/ECE 
standards, cover head completely, and be properly fastened. 
●​ Car Seatbelts: Mandatory for all occupants; children under 14 must use appropriate 
restraints. 
●​ Exemptions: Pregnant women with medical certificate for seatbelts; limited exemptions for 
helmets in specific categories.
