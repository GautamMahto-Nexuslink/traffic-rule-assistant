8.1.1 Driving Related Violations 
●​ Driving Without License: ₹5,000 (first offense), ₹10,000 (subsequent); imprisonment up to 3 
months. 
●​ Driving Under Influence: ₹10,000 (first offense), ₹15,000 (subsequent); imprisonment up to 
6 months (first), 2 years (subsequent); license suspension 6 months to 2 years. 
●​ Rash/Negligent Driving: ₹5,000; imprisonment up to 6 months. 
●​ Overspeeding: 
○​ Light motor vehicles: ₹1,000-₹2,000 (first offense), ₹2,000-₹4,000 (subsequent). 
○​ Medium passenger/goods vehicle: ₹2,000-₹4,000. 
○​ Heavy passenger/goods vehicle: ₹3,000-₹5,000. 
○​ Dangerous speeding: Imprisonment up to 6 months; license suspension for repeat 
offenders.
