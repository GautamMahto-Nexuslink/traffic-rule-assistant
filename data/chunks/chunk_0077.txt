Give Way Line

Diagram 27

The give way line is usually a double dotted line marked
transversely at junctions. These lines are generally
supplemented by a reverse triangle ‘give way' sign painted on the
road surface before the dotted lines or by a road sign installed
beside the marking. Give way line indicates that you should give
way to traffic on the main approaching road.

by 'Pedestrian Crossing’ road signs.
