6.3. Turning

Turning improperly or without adequate indication may result ina
traffic accident. Move safely to the proper lane well ahead of your
turn. Always follow MSM rule while changing to the turn lane and
while making a turn. Turn only when it is safe to do so. While
turning left, look for the merging traffic from the right side. Look
for oncoming traffic while turning right. Finish the turn in the
proper lane. If you need to move to another lane, do so only after
completing you turn and then change the lane following the MSM
rule. If the intersection is controlled by the traffic lights or by the
traffic police personnel turn only when your lane is given the
proper signal to turn (see Diagrams from 71 to 82).
