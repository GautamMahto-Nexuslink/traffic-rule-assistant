6.2.1 Learner's License 
●​ Eligibility: 
○​ Minimum age: 16 years for MCWOG, 18 years for other categories, 20 years for 
transport vehicles. 
○​ Must not be disqualified from holding a license. 
○​ Must pass vision test and written examination on traffic rules and road signs. 
●​ Documents Required: 
○​ Age proof (birth certificate, school certificate, passport). 
○​ Address proof (Aadhaar, voter ID, passport, utility bill). 
○​ Medical certificate (for commercial licenses, from authorized doctor). 
○​ Passport-size photographs (3 copies). 
○​ Application fee payment receipt (₹150). 
●​ Restrictions: 
○​ Must display 'L' board while driving. 
○​ Cannot drive on national highways. 

○​ Must be accompanied by a licensed driver. 
○​ Cannot carry passengers (except instructor). 
●​ Validity: 6 months from date of issue.
