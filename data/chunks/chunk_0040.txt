cannot drive on national highways or carry passengers (except instructor). ○​ Q2: What is the minimum age for getting a driving license?​ A: 16 years for motorcycles without gear, 18 years for other categories, 20 years for transport vehicles. ○​ Q3: Can I apply for multiple vehicle categories together?​ A: No, each category requires a separate application and test. ○​ Q4: What happens if I don’t renew my license on time?​ A: You can renew within 1 year of expiry with a late fee; after 1 year, a fresh application is required.
