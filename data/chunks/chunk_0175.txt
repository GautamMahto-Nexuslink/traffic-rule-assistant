6.2.2 Permanent License 
●​ Eligibility: 
○​ Must hold valid learner's license for minimum 30 days. 
○​ Must pass practical driving test. 
●​ Documents Required: 
○​ Valid learner's license. 
○​ Driving test appointment confirmation. 
○​ Form 2 (application for driving license). 
○​ Medical certificate (if required, for transport vehicles). 
●​ Test Requirements: Practical driving test. 
●​ Validity: 
○​ Non-transport vehicles: 20 years or until age 50, then renewable every 5 years. 
○​ Transport vehicles: 3 years initially, then renewable every 5 years.
