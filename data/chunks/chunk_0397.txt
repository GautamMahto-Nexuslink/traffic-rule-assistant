10.3 Complaint and Grievance Procedure 
●​ Direct Complaint: To traffic police station. 
●​ Online Complaints: Through department website (pgportal.gov.in) or mobile apps. 
●​ Mobile Applications: Dedicated apps for complaints (e.g., mParivahan). 
●​ Helpline Numbers: 24/7 complaint helplines (e.g., 103 for traffic, 1091 for women’s helpline). 
●​ Citizen Reporting: 
○​ Online complaint systems and traffic violation reporting apps. 
○​ Reward schemes for reporting. 
○​ Anonymous reporting options. 
●​ Investigation Process: 
○​ Initial assessment and evidence collection. 
○​ Officer inquiry and resolution based on findings.
