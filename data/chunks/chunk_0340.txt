5.2.2 Warning Signs (Triangular with Red Border) 
●​ Sharp Turn: Diamond-shaped yellow signs for right/left curves or hairpin bends. 
●​ School Zone: Children crossing warnings. 
●​ Animal Crossing: Wildlife warning signs. 
●​ Road Work: Construction zone warnings. 
●​ Cross Road/Side Road: Intersection or side road warnings. 
●​ Narrow Road/Bridge: Road narrows ahead.
