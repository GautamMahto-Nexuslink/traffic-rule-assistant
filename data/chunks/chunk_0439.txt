14.2 Electric Vehicle Promotion 
●​ Policy Support: 
○​ Subsidies for EV purchase. 
○​ Tax benefits (reduced registration and road tax). 
○​ Charging station development. 
○​ Simplified permit procedures. 
●​ Regulatory Framework: 
○​ Safety standards for EVs. 
○​ Environmentally safe battery disposal. 
○​ Standards for charging stations. 
○​ Grid integration for power supply.
