3.1 Rule-Making Authority 
The Tamil Nadu Motor Vehicles Rules 1989 were framed under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988 to 
provide detailed procedures and requirements for: 
●​ Vehicle registration procedures. 
●​ Driving license issuance and renewal. 
●​ Permit systems for commercial vehicles. 
●​ Safety standards and inspections. 
●​ Fee structures and documentation requirements.
